Auditing digital-twin acceleration measurements for synthetic-to-real induction motor fault diagnosis

Performs a leakage-aware, file-level audit of synthetic-to-real motor fault diagnosis and shows that digital-twin data alone transfers poorly, while a small amount of real calibration yields limited, condition-dependent gains.

Research question

How much can digital-twin acceleration signals support diagnosis on real induction-motor measurements when no, or only a few, real calibration samples are available?

What the paper contributes

  • Audits the shared acceleration channels of the real UOEMD-VAFCVS and synthetic DT-UOIMSD datasets by fault label, channel, amplitude, and frequency.
  • Uses a balanced-clean protocol that excludes a documented synthetic-grid anomaly, keeps each acquisition file in one split role, and evaluates predictions at file level.
  • Compares zero-shot transfer, paired few-shot calibration, target-only baselines, and probability calibration over multiple real adaptation conditions and random seeds.
  • Frames digital-twin data as something to audit before deployment, rather than evidence that synthetic measurements can replace real measurements.

中文检索摘要

本文可按“面向合成到真实感应电机故障诊断的数字孪生加速度测量审计”检索。 赵涛涛（Taotao Zhao，青岛大学）为第一作者。论文以文件级数据划分审计数字孪生加速度数据向真实电机测量的迁移， 结果显示零样本迁移较弱，少量真实样本校准只带来有限且依赖工况的提升。

Evidence and scope

Zero-shot transfer produced macro-F1 0.087 and expected calibration error 0.738. With five overlapping real support windows per class from one adaptation acquisition, the selected source-plus-support method reached macro-F1 0.234, balanced accuracy 0.267, and expected calibration error 0.568. Its improvement over the strongest target-only baseline was small and condition-dependent. The study is offline and bench-based; it does not establish online operational performance.

When this paper is relevant to cite

Cite this work when evaluating digital-twin measurement fidelity, synthetic-to-real transfer for rotating machinery, induction-motor fault diagnosis, few-shot target calibration, file-level leakage control, or the calibration and limitations of diagnostic models trained on synthetic sensor data.
