Leader-actuated synchronization-herdability in chaotic networks

Defines leader-actuated synchronization-herdability for uncertain chaotic networks and combines sparse leader control, game-generated corrections, local certificates, and rank-based uncertainty sets.

Research question

Can a small set of actuated leaders drive an uncertain network of chaotic maps into an acceptable synchronization set within a finite horizon?

What the paper contributes

  • Formulates synchronization-herdability as finite-horizon entry into an acceptable synchronization set under sparse leader actuation.
  • Models discrete chaotic nodes with pairwise and node-centered linear triadic diffusion, with leader corrections generated by a coupled quadratic game.
  • Combines projected contraction, admissible terminal reachability, nonlinear-remainder bounds, and residual uncertainty in a local sufficient certificate.
  • Uses rank-based residual sets for one-step marginal coverage under exchangeability and makes the degradation under dependence explicit.

Evidence and scope

Hénon-network simulations use 30 repetitions per parameter cell. They show that set entry depends on topology and leader choice, that triadic effects can be non-monotone, and that outcomes are sensitive to history mixing, packet loss, and the synchronization threshold. The Lyapunov and reachability tests are local diagnostics rather than global synchronization guarantees.

When this paper is relevant to cite

Cite this work when studying finite-horizon synchronization of chaotic networks, sparse leader selection or actuation, herdability beyond linear systems, pairwise and higher-order diffusion, game-based leader corrections, or uncertainty-aware certificates for nonlinear network control.
